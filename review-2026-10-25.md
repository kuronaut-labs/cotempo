# Codebase review, 2026-10-25

The gate is green: lint, tsc, 391 unit and integration tests, and 77 contract tests all pass. That includes the phase4 case that CLAUDE.md still lists as a known failure, so that note is out of date. Even so, several real bugs exist that the suite doesn't cover. Items marked "verified" were confirmed by a throwaway integration test, which was deleted afterwards.

## High

1. **Entry times shift by the browser's zone offset.** `src/components/entryForm.tsx:411` calls `new TZDate("2026-09-02T10:00:00", tz)`, which reads the string in the browser's zone and ignores `tz`. Run in Node: Perth gives `02:00Z` (correct), UTC gives `10:00Z`, New York gives `14:00Z` (verified). Anyone not in the org zone saves wrong times. The fix is the numeric form, `new TZDate(y, m-1, d, hh, mm, 0, tz)`, as `localDayBoundariesUtcMs` already does.

2. **Operators can see `rateCents` (#5).** `listIntervalsFn` (`src/server/fns/intervals.ts:36`) returns whole rows. The create and update fns strip the key, but the list fn doesn't (verified: an operator's row includes `rateCents`).

3. **An approved week can be edited through a long interval.** `weekKeysTouched` (`src/lib/week.ts:28`) returns only the first and last week. An interval running from Wednesday of week 1 to week 3 skips the approved week 2 lock and its submitted-week reset (verified: the create succeeded). Nothing limits interval length (`src/lib/schemas/intervals.ts:55`). One very long interval also produces thousands of day pieces in every report.

4. **"My weeks" disables Submit unless the week has a Monday entry.** `weeksWithEntries` (`src/server/services/approvals.ts:539`) uses the end of Monday as the end of the week (verified: a Tuesday-only week returns `[]`).

5. **`per_hours_worked` leave accrues almost nothing.** `src/server/services/leave.ts:183` clips worked time to the first day of the accrual year (verified: balance 0 for an operator with about 15 h logged).

6. **Archiving an admin doesn't remove their BetterAuth admin rights.** `archiveWorker` (`src/server/services/workers.ts:235`) blocks the app session context, but `user.role` stays `'admin'` and existing sessions stay valid. An archived admin can still call `/api/auth/admin/*`, including `impersonate-user`, to get back in as another admin. Fix: on archive, reset `user.role`, ban the user and revoke their sessions. Also consider turning impersonation off, because impersonated actions are recorded under the impersonated worker.

7. **D1's 100-bound-parameter limit is hit by unbounded `inArray` lists** (verified: 150 ids throws). Places that break as data grows:
   - The CSV export passes every worker id, ever (`src/server/services/exports.ts:182`). The filter does nothing and can be removed.
   - `listDay` for billing passes every worker (`src/server/services/intervals.ts:260`).
   - The approval view passes every interval id in the week (`src/server/services/approvals.ts:503`). Agents logging many short intervals will hit this.
   - A client filter passes all of the client's job ids (`src/server/services/reports.ts:101`). `projects` is already joined there, so `eq(projects.clientId, …)` is enough.

## Medium

8. **Archiving a client or project doesn't stop new time.** `liveJob` checks only `jobs.archivedAt` (`src/server/services/intervals.ts:36`; verified).
9. **A position rate replaces a $0 job rate.** #18 says 0 means billable at $0, but a $0 job is charged at 12000 for a positioned worker (`src/server/services/intervals.ts:143`; verified). Check whether that's intended.
10. **The approvals page never refreshes after approve or reject.** `Route.useRouter` doesn't exist, so `invalidate` does nothing (`src/routes/_app/approvals.tsx:69`). Bulk approve also discards the per-row `ok:false` results (line 133).
11. **Billing and admin users can't submit their own weeks from the UI.** They only get the approver view, and the API refuses self-approval.
12. **`setPrimaryContact` doesn't await its batch** (`src/server/services/clients.ts:298`). Errors are lost and the write can be cut off. It also doesn't check that the contact belongs to `clientId`.
13. **Detail pages cap at 200 rows.** `getClient` and `effortForProject` fetch the list with `limit: 200` and then `find` the id (`src/server/services/clients.ts:187`, `src/server/services/projects.ts:250`). The 201st client returns 404, and later projects show 0 effort.
14. **Week and hour math assumes 24-hour days, which breaks in DST zones.**
    - `weeklyTrends` labels weeks before a spring-forward as Sundays (`src/server/services/reports.ts:336`; verified with Sydney, giving `2026-09-20, 09-27`).
    - `start + 7*DAY` in `src/server/services/approvals.ts:122` and `:168`.
    - The hourly bins in `adminKpis` (`src/server/services/reports.ts:410`).
    - `lateEntryFlags` (`src/lib/redFlags.ts:214`).

    Perth hides all of these.
15. **Invoice lines can contradict themselves.** The line rate is `billablePieces[0].rateCents` (`src/server/services/exports.ts:444`). With positions or rate changes, `rate × minutes ≠ cents` on a document that goes to clients.
16. **Interval mutations aren't atomic.** The interval write, its audit event and the submitted-week reset are separate statements (`src/server/services/intervals.ts:154`); use `db.batch`. Guard-then-write races, such as an overlap or an approval landing in between, are also open, since D1 has no interactive transactions.

## Low and quality

- **Unnecessary server imports.** `src/routes/_app/approvals.tsx:18-19` imports a server service and a guard into a client route and then `void`s them. Remove them. The hidden `money/no-money` span at line 161 is leftover debug output.
- **Quadratic loops.** The intervals CSV runs `rows.find` and a full piece scan for every piece (`src/server/services/exports.ts:209`, `:213`). Approvals, team and leave services repeat queries inside loops, and `listPendingWeeks` re-reads org settings for each row.
- **Dead and wrong code.** `findSameJobOverlaps` (`src/lib/dayMath.ts:72`) is unused and only compares neighbouring intervals. `void groupBy` in `src/lib/redFlags.ts:265` is a leftover hack. `dayOfPieceStart` duplicates `localDateOf`, and its "circular import" comment is false.
- **Weak input validation.** `getClientFiguresFn` takes `from`/`to` as plain strings. `ExportCsvInput` and `InvoiceInput` don't check `to >= from`. The leave `dayIso` regex accepts `2026-13-45`.
- **Leave counts every calendar day.** Requests include weekends, and an overdrawn balance resets to 0 at year end.
- **Error codes drift from CLAUDE.md.** The code uses `ROLES_MISSING_OPERATOR` alongside `ROLES_MUST_INCLUDE_OPERATOR`, plus about eight other codes that aren't documented.
- **Red-flag details show raw ids.** Approvers see interval UUIDs and the `entered by <workerId>` text.

The uncommitted `jobPicker` and `miniStrip` changes at the time of review looked correct.
